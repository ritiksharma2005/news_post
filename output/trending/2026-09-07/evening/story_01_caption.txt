🔥 DRDO Invites Startups To Build Microwave Anti Drone Tech

📝 What Happened:
DRDO is inviting Indian startups and MSMEs to develop SHIELD, a high-power microwave system that tests how radiation neutralizes drone electronics. This builds on an earlier prototype that successfully brought down small quadcopters from a kilometer away.
