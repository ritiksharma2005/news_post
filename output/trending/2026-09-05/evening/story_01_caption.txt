🔥 India Celebrates Teachers Day Honoring Dr Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan

📝 What Happened:
Observed annually on September 5, the occasion marks the birth anniversary of former President and philosopher Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan. The day highlights the foundational role teachers play in fostering intellect, discipline, and future leaders across India.
