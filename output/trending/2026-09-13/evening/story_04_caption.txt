🔥 Sandbags Found Blocking Varanasi Sewers, Sabotage Suspected

📝 What Happened:
Over a dozen sandbags and debris were discovered inside main sewer lines in Varanasi, causing severe sewage overflow across several wards. Municipal officials suspect deliberate sabotage and have requested an FIR while reviewing CCTV footage to identify culprits.
