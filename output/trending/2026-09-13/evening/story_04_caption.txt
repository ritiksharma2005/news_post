🔥 WHO Warns India Of Rising Dengue And Heat Risk

📝 What Happened:
The World Health Organization has warned that persistent El Niño weather patterns could trigger a spike in dengue and heat-related illnesses across India. Experts expect these unpredictable climate conditions to continue through early 2027.
