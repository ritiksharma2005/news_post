🔥 India's Forex Reserves Surge To Record $785 Billion

📝 What Happened:
India’s foreign exchange reserves reached an all-time high of $785.71 billion thanks to a massive influx of non-resident Indian deposits. This record surge provides a strong financial cushion against currency volatility and rising global crude oil prices.
