# NEWS NIT IIT Storytelling Formula & Rules

## 1. Core Principles
1. **Never read a caption word-for-word**: Synthesize facts into a fast, punchy broadcast news report.
2. **Hook within the first 3 seconds**: Open with a provocative question, contrasting stat, or surprising name drop.
3. **Pacing**: Speak at ~145–160 WPM (+12% rate on Edge-TTS `en-IN-PrabhatNeural`). Total word count: 65–95 words.
4. **Letter-by-Letter Pronunciation**:
   - Always spell out: **`N - I - T`** and **`I - I - T`**.
   - In prompts and scripts, write as hyphenated capitals (`N-I-T`, `I-I-T`) so TTS engines never blend them into words.

---

## 2. Five-Part Narrative Architecture

### Part 1: The Hook (0–5 sec | ~12–15 words)
- *Goal*: Grab attention before the user scrolls past.
- *Format*: Question or bold assertion highlighting top brands or numbers.
- *Example*: *"What do CoinDCX, Blinkit, Ola, and BrowserStack all have in common?"*

### Part 2: The Reveal (5–15 sec | ~18–25 words)
- *Goal*: Answer the hook and introduce the core development.
- *Format*: Connect the entities to their alma mater or achievement.
- *Example*: *"They are all built by entrepreneurs from the I-I-T Bombay alumni network."*

### Part 3: The Deep Dive (15–27 sec | ~25–30 words)
- *Goal*: Provide concrete proof, sectors, and specifics.
- *Format*: Rapid-fire listing of diverse sectors (Fintech, SaaS, DeepTech, Quick Commerce) and unicorns.
- *Example*: *"From SaaS pioneers like Druva to consumer powerhouses like Cars24 and NoBroker, the footprint spans every major sector."*

### Part 4: The Bigger Picture (27–35 sec | ~15–20 words)
- *Goal*: Elevate the story to national or campus significance.
- *Format*: Visionary takeaway on innovation and leadership.
- *Example*: *"This is not just about startup numbers — it proves how India's top tech institutes are driving the national economic engine."*

### Part 5: The Outro (35–40 sec | ~10 words)
- *Goal*: Brand sign-off and call to action.
- *Mandatory Wording*:
  `"This is News N-I-T, I-I-T — your campus, our news."`
